# One Blog

Welcome to my personal digital garden. I write about ideas, experiments, and lessons from software engineering, AI tools, personal knowledge management, and product practice.

> [!abstract] What is this place?
> It is not a chronological feed that disappears after being read. It is a growing set of public notes that remain connected and are revised as my work develops.

## Topics

- **Software engineering**: development methods, engineering productivity, quality, and automation.
- **AI and tools**: AI-assisted workflows, tool design, and practical experience.
- **Knowledge management**: Obsidian, bidirectional links, and knowledge-base governance.
- **Product practice**: from problem discovery and solution design to delivery reviews.

## Publishing principle

Only notes explicitly marked with `publish: true` in frontmatter appear here. The original vault directory structure remains unchanged, and private notes never enter the site build input.

> [!tip]
> Use the explorer, search, and graph to navigate public notes. They remain within the selected language unless you explicitly follow a language fallback.
